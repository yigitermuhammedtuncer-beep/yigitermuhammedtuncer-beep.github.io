Course 059250 — Soft Robotics

Bio-Inspired Soft Gripper

An underactuated, tendon-driven soft gripper designed to harvest button mushrooms without bruising them — and one motor away from grasping almost anything else on a table.

Institution Politecnico di Milano
Timeline June 2026
Team Group 7 — 5 members
My role CAD design, tribo-skin mold & casting, circuit & testing

The final build — three underactuated fingers driven by a single servo through a whiffletree.

Context & Problem

Why mushrooms are a hard grasping problem

Button mushrooms sit at the difficult end of robotic manipulation: they're irregular, asymmetrical, and damage easily under even modest point loads.

Constraint 01

Low bruising threshold

Past a small pressure limit, the cap tissue ruptures and browns almost immediately — there's very little margin for error in grip force.

Constraint 02

Irregular geometry

No two caps are the same shape, so a rigid, pre-programmed grasp point tends to concentrate force in exactly the wrong place.

The failure of rigid automation in agriculture isn't a speed problem — it's a compliance problem.
Motivation & Approach

From top-down suction to a wrapping grasp

Existing mushroom-harvesting robots mostly fall into two camps, chosen by mushroom type: suction cups that lift from the cap, and small pincers that cut at the stem. Both have known failure modes — suction clogs with soil and spores and bruises the cap with localized negative pressure; stem-cutters need precise, per-mushroom alignment.

We moved away from both, toward a three-fingered wrapping motion that closes gently around the cap and distributes pressure across the whole contact surface rather than a single point — closer to how a hand cups a fragile object than how a suction nozzle grabs one.

The Forager HX800 — the commercial mushroom-picking robot whose optimized top-down trimming motion we studied, and moved away from, toward a wrapping grasp.
Goal 01

Passive adaptation

Let the finger geometry itself do the conforming, instead of adding sensor feedback loops — the material is the controller.

Goal 02

Pressure homogenization

Spread contact stress evenly across the cap surface so no single point takes enough load to bruise it.

Goal 03

Underactuated simplicity

Drive all three fingers from a single motor, keeping the mechanism — and its failure modes — simple.

Research question: how can an underactuated soft morphology combine force-isotropic tendon routing with tuned surface friction to achieve a secure, zero-bruise grasp on irregular, sensitive targets?
CAD Design

Tendon routing, tribo-skin, and finger geometry

We compared serial and parallel tendon routing before settling on a single-actuator whiffletree — one cable pulling three fingers through a small triangular plate, keeping the pulling force isotropic across all three.

To increase grip without increasing clamp force, we gave the fingertips a pyramidal surface texture — a tribo-skin — which maximizes contact area and friction. It's the same idea used in fingerprint-inspired soft-robot skins: more surface contact, more friction, without pressing any harder.

Cast pyramidal tribo-skin texture on a fingertip — the pattern that increases friction and grip without added clamp force.

The finger itself went through two iterations. The first version used a rigid segmented cross-section; the final version replaced it with a continuous variable cross-section — softer where it needs to bend, stiffer where it needs to hold — closing more like a real finger and gripping small, round objects without them slipping out.

Full assembly — servo and whiffletree at the top, three tendon-driven fingers below.
Circuit

A four-part control loop

Digital servo and pulley — the single actuator that drives all three fingers through the whiffletree.
Input

Potentiometer

A twistable knob acting as a variable resistor, giving manual control over how far the fingers close.

Controller

Arduino Uno

Reads the potentiometer voltage, converts it into a target angle, and sends the servo a control signal.

Actuator

Digital servo

Rotates its arm on command, pulling the tendon through the whiffletree and closing the fingers.

Power

4× AA batteries

Supplies 6V directly to the servo, kept isolated from the Arduino's own supply.

Materials & Methods

Why the fingers are cast in two layers

No single silicone solves every constraint at once, so each finger is cast from two different materials doing two different jobs.

Early casting tests — comparing Smooth-Sil (blue) against Dragon Skin (white) as the finger's structural body.
PropertySmooth-Sil bodyDragon Skin body
StiffnessHigh — rigid, needs more force to wrapMedium — flexes smoothly around objects
Load behaviourLifts heavier objects without bucklingHolds its structure without tearing
StatusPromising for a future heavy-lift versionChosen baseline — worked well on the first cast

For the skin layer, Ecoflex 00-35 Fast reproduced the fine pyramidal tribo-skin texture far more cleanly than the stiffer Ecoflex 10, which struggled to fill the small mold cavities and left the pattern soft and rounded.

The result is a two-layer finger: a Dragon Skin core that flexes passively and resists buckling under load, wrapped in an ultra-soft, high-friction Ecoflex 00-35 skin that grips gently but securely.

Limitations & Next Steps

Where it still fails, and what would fix it

Failure mode

Objects fall through the gap

Anything smaller than the finger aperture can slip into the dead zone between fingers instead of being caught.

Failure mode

Weak fingertip grip

The tip has no Dragon Skin backbone behind it, so it can't press hard enough to generate reliable friction on its own.

Failure mode

Off-center contact

Gripping above an object's center of mass makes the hold unstable on rigid, tapered shapes.

Planned improvements: add soft webbing between fingers to close the dead zone, deepen the tip texture so it grips with less applied force, route tendons through low-friction tubing to preserve more of the servo's pulling force, and offer a stiffer Smooth-Sil finger variant for heavier, rigid loads.
