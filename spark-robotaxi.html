Overview

As part of KU AIRS SPARK, Koç University's team for TEKNOFEST's Full-Scale Autonomous Vehicle Competition, I helped build a fully autonomous car from scratch for the "unique vehicle" category. We were the university's first-ever entrants in the competition, so there was no prior hardware, documentation, or institutional experience to build on — the whole platform was designed and manufactured from a blank sheet. Pandemic-related lab restrictions also meant the team only had physical access to build and test the car in the final two months before the event.

Despite that compressed timeline, the car was completed and the team was selected as a finalist at TEKNOFEST 2021, finishing 6th out of 30 competing teams.

My role: I was responsible for the mechanical design of individual structural parts and for the overall design of the vehicle's outer shell — shaping both how the car was built and how it looked.
